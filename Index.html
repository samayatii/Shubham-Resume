Professional Profile

I work in the Data & Analytics function with a focus on Master Data Management (MDM). My role involves handling business/client master-data requests from initial data receipt through validation, cleansing, mapping, exception resolution, quality checks, stakeholder coordination and final closure.

My work covers Stockist/Distributor Master, Product/Item Master and Company-to-Distributor (C2D/Trusale) mapping, along with GST validation, duplicate identification, A-code activities, master-data corrections and QC. I also contribute to process-improvement discussions and BRD requirements for improving the stockist mapping experience.

I work across multiple client datasets and coordinate with internal teams, reviewers and stakeholders to clarify exceptions, resolve data-quality issues and deliver accurate outputs.

End-to-End MDM Workflow

1. Client / Business Team → Data or Mapping Request Received
2. Requirement Understanding → Scope, fields, client rules & deadline
3. Excel/Data Preparation → Cleaning, standardisation, formatting & duplicate checks
4. MDM Validation → Compare client data with existing master records
5. Classification → Existing / New / Duplicate / Update / Exception
6. Mapping / Master Update → Stockist, Product, C2D or Trusale activity
7. Exception Resolution → GST, address, code, source, duplicate & status issues
8. QC → Count reconciliation, field-level validation & mapping accuracy
9. Stakeholder Review → Feedback, clarification & correction
10. Final Submission → Updated file / mapping / master output & statistics
11. Closure → Confirmation, documentation & next activity

Stockist / Distributor Master – Detailed Work

Data Intake

Receive stockist lists, mapping files, new-code requests, correction requests and client master files from business/client stakeholders.

Data Preparation

Review stockist code, firm name, address, city, district, state, pincode, GST and other available attributes; clean and standardise data.

Master Matching

Search and compare client records against MDM/stockist master using GST, code, name, address and location attributes.

New / Existing / Duplicate

Identify existing stockists, potential new records, duplicates and records requiring further confirmation.

GST Validation

Use GST as a key validation attribute; handle missing GST, mismatch, GST-already-exists cases and address differences according to business rules.

A-Code Activities

Support new A-code creation/update requests where required and validate mandatory information before processing.

Duplicate Management

Investigate duplicate cases, confirm the active/original code with the relevant team, and route cases to Tech/concerned stakeholders when required.

Location Validation

Validate state, city, district and pincode; identify client-vs-master location mismatches and raise clarification where necessary.

Product / Item Master – Detailed Work

Product Data Intake

Handle client/product files and item-mapping requests received for master-data operations.

Product Validation

Review available product/item attributes and compare client information with existing master records.

Item Mapping

Identify mapped and unmapped items, perform appropriate mapping activities and maintain accuracy across master relationships.

Exception Handling

Identify missing, inconsistent or duplicate product information and coordinate for clarification or correction.

QC & Reconciliation

Validate final counts, mapped/unmapped records and output consistency before closure.

Stockist + Product Mapping

Once the relevant stockist/distributor and product/item records are validated, I handle the mapping workflow by checking existing relationships, identifying unmapped combinations, performing manual mapping where applicable, handling exceptions and completing QC before final submission.

C2D / Trusale Mapping

Company → Distributor mapping: receive company/client data, validate company and distributor information, check existing relationships, identify unmapped records, perform manual mapping where applicable, handle rejection/exception scenarios, reconcile results and share final statistics/output.

This includes working with relevant MDM mapping datasets and coordinating with stakeholders when source information, codes or mapping status requires clarification.

Data Analytics & SQL Workflow

01 · Data Extraction

Use SQL/Snowflake queries to extract relevant Stockist, Distributor, Product, Company-to-Distributor and mapping datasets from available MDM tables. Identify required columns, filter records by client, source, status, date or business requirement, and prepare the working dataset.

02 · Data Profiling

Review record counts, null values, duplicate GST/PAN/code records, missing mandatory attributes, inconsistent names, addresses, city/state/district and mapping status before starting the operational activity.

03 · Data Cleaning

Use Advanced Excel and Power BI data-transformation capabilities to clean, standardize and structure the data. Handle blanks, inconsistent formats, duplicate records, naming variations and location-related mismatches before validation.

04 · Data Validation

Compare client data with MDM/master data and validate GST, PAN, stockist codes, product attributes, company details, addresses and existing mapping status. Use SQL-based checks and Excel reconciliation to identify exceptions.

05 · Analysis & Reporting

Prepare mapped, unmapped, duplicate, pending and exception counts. Use Power BI/Excel for analysis, summaries, trend views, status tracking and management-ready reporting where required.

06 · Business Action

Convert the validated analysis into mapping, creation, update, duplicate-tagging or clarification actions in the relevant MDM workflow, followed by QC and final reporting.
